# Product: Prehendo

Prehendo ("to grasp" in Latin) is a detective mini game that teaches junior high
students (ages 13–16) to spot fake news through simulation-based learning.

## Users
- **Student**: plays without an account (nickname only). Investigates simulated posts,
  ticks red flags, calls a verdict (Real / Fake / Misleading), and gets coached.
- **Teacher**: registers and logs in. Designs cases with AI help and approves every
  case before students see it.

## Principles
- **AI suggests, humans decide.** The teacher sets the answer key (blueprint); the AI only
  writes the story. Nothing AI-written reaches students without teacher approval.
- **Fictional world only.** Every case happens in the made-up city of Puerto Liwanag.
  No real people, brands, or news events. No AI-generated photos.
- **Protect minors.** Students store nothing but a nickname, kept in their own browser.
- **Open by design.** MIT-licensed code; only open-weight models (Gemma 4, GPT-OSS).
